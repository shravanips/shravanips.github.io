Exploring prompt injection, jailbreak techniques, and adversarial evaluation methods to understand how language-model systems behave under malicious or conflicting instructions.

Overview

My current research interests focus on understanding how adversarial instructions can influence language-model behavior, especially when AI systems interact with untrusted content, external tools, or conflicting instructions. I am reviewing existing research and developing questions around attack generation, evaluation, and robustness.

Current Research Directions

  • Reviewing research on prompt injection, jailbreak techniques, and automated adversarial attack generation.
  • Studying reinforcement learning approaches for generating and evaluating prompt injection attacks.
  • Exploring how attack effectiveness, transferability, and adaptive defenses can be evaluated systematically.
