Learning-Aware Fault Tolerance in Distributed Reinforcement Learning Systems

IntelliSys Lab · Stevens Institute of Technology

← All research
Oct 2025 – May 2026

Controlled worker-failure experiments exploring how partial failures affect training stability, convergence, latency, and reliability.

Overview

This research examined how distributed reinforcement learning systems behave when training workers fail. Using Ray RLlib and PPO, I designed controlled experiments across MuJoCo and Atari environments to investigate the effects of partial worker loss on training performance and system reliability.

Research Contributions

  • Built a Python and Ray RLlib experimental framework for distributed reinforcement learning and worker-failure scenarios.
  • Evaluated PPO training across MuJoCo and Atari environments.
  • Tracked reward progression, convergence behavior, checkpoint overhead, and iteration latency.
  • Investigated training resilience and performance bottlenecks under partial worker failures.
