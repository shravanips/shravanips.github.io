SHRAVANI / A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

A Little More About Me

A little about my journey, the things I'm curious about, and life beyond the screen.

01 / THE PERSON

Hi, I'm Shravani!

I've always been curious about the things that aren't immediately obvious. Why something works the way it does, what happens when it doesn't, and what we can learn by looking a little closer.

I think that curiosity is what brought me to where I am today.

I started out studying forensic science in Mumbai, where I became interested in digital evidence, investigations, and understanding how technology can be used, misused, or misunderstood. Eventually, that interest led me to cybersecurity and then to computer science at Stevens Institute of Technology in New Jersey.

Moving from forensics to cybersecurity and now exploring artificial intelligence wasn't something I had planned from the beginning. Each step introduced me to new questions, and those questions led me somewhere else.

One thing I've realized along the way is that I enjoy research not just because it involves finding answers, but because it gives me the freedom to ask questions I hadn't thought of before. I like taking an unfamiliar problem, breaking it down, experimenting, getting things wrong, and slowly making sense of it.

But there's more to me than research and computers. I enjoy writing about my experiences, reading books, exploring places, and capturing moments through photography. I also like discovering different cultures, learning languages, and occasionally going down a rabbit hole about something completely unrelated to what I'm supposed to be doing.

I don't think all my interests need to connect to one another. Some things are worth exploring simply because they're interesting.

I'm still figuring out what I want to build, which questions I want to spend years investigating, and where those interests might take me. For now, I'm enjoying the process of learning, experimenting, and finding my own direction.

Perhaps that's what this website is about, too — a little space on the internet where I can document that journey.
